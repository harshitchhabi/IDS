\section{CICIDS2017 Is Near-Degenerate}
\label{sec:degeneracy}
% DECISIONS.md s16: under CICFlowMeter flow-summary features CICIDS2017 is low-entropy in BOTH
% classes -- every family with usable volume, and benign, has a near-identical twin in the
% training-side pool no matter how the data is split. Table source:
% results/phase0/cicids/nn_degeneracy_by_grid.csv (median per-feature RMS NN distance,
% trusted_eval -> honeypot_pool, guard-(c) metric, four dedup grids).
Before any attack or defense result can mean anything, the dataset it is measured on has to be
able to carry the claim. It cannot, on CICIDS2017, in the feature representation
CICFlowMeter produces. Every family with usable volume, and benign traffic itself, has a
near-identical twin on the other side of any partition boundary we tried.

% DECISIONS.md s11, s16: guard-(a) grid sweep {0.005, 0.01, 0.02, 0.05}; median NN RMS distance
% stays 14x+ below the 0.25 guard-(c) threshold at every grid for every family with >=500
% trusted_eval rows, benign included (5th percentile 0.0007-0.0058 across the same grids).
Table~\ref{tab:degeneracy} reports the median nearest-neighbour distance, in normalized
feature space, from each held-out \texttt{trusted\_eval} row to its nearest same-class
\texttt{honeypot\_pool} row, at four near-duplicate-removal grid resolutions. Every family with
at least 500 \texttt{trusted\_eval} rows — the bar below which a rate cannot be reported
stably — sits at least $14\times$ below the 0.25 leakage threshold at every grid, benign
included (5th percentile 0.0007–0.0058 across the same range). Coarser deduplication moves the
distances only by deleting rows; none approaches the threshold.

% DECISIONS.md s16: shuffled-partition control (phase0_degeneracy_control.py) -- pool each
% family's rows across all three partitions, shuffle uniformly, re-split at the same sizes.
% Real vs shuffled median NN agree within ~1.5x for benign and every high-volume family
% (BENIGN 0.0067 vs 0.0078, DDoS 0.0049 vs 0.0038, DoS Hulk 0.0054 vs 0.0040), so no partition
% -- temporal, burst-level, or otherwise -- can separate train from eval for these families.
This is not an artifact of where the split falls. Pooling each family's rows across all three
partitions, shuffling uniformly, and re-splitting at the same sizes reproduces the same
distances to within a factor of 1.5 for benign and every high-volume family: BENIGN 0.0067 vs.\
0.0078 shuffled, DDoS 0.0049 vs.\ 0.0038, DoS Hulk 0.0054 vs.\ 0.0040. No partition — burst-aware
or otherwise (Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix}) — can separate train from held-out here, because the
distances are set by the feature distribution, not by the cut.

% DECISIONS.md s15: no family clears both bars (>=500 eval rows AND non-degenerate). The
% "structured" families (Bot, Heartbleed, Infiltration, Web Attack - Sql Injection) all have
% <500 eval rows (Bot is the largest at 193).
No family clears both the volume bar and a non-degenerate distance. Figure~\ref{fig:f2-nn-by-family}
shows every family with usable volume sits left of the threshold; the only families that sit to
its right (Heartbleed, Bot, DoS slowloris near it) all have under 500 \texttt{trusted\_eval}
rows — Bot, the largest, has 193. There is no family that is simultaneously voluminous enough to
report a stable rate on and distinguishable enough across a partition boundary to demonstrate
learning rather than memorization.

% Writing rule (PAPER_PLAN.md): do not claim any S0 learning result on CICIDS. DECISIONS.md
% s16, s18: consequence -- CICIDS answers the degeneracy measurement and the A1
% damage-vs-distance curve (A1's poison is benign rows stamped malicious; its damage is a
% function of a distance we set and measure, and degeneracy hands us the near end of that curve
% for free); synthetic (entropy controlled by construction, guard (c) clean) answers S0.
\textbf{We draw no S0 learning claim from CICIDS2017.} A detector "improving" on this data
would be rewarded for recognizing near-copies of specific held-out rows, not for learning a
boundary that generalizes to traffic the loop never saw. CICIDS2017 instead answers two things
this paper needs: the degeneracy measurement itself, and A1's damage-versus-distance curve in
Section~\ref{sec:attack} — A1's poison is benign traffic stamped malicious, its damage is a
function of a distance we set and directly measure, and this section's near-duplicate rate
hands us the near end of that curve for free. S0's learning claim (Section~\ref{sec:attack}) is
made on the synthetic dataset instead, where entropy is controlled by construction and guard (c)
passes cleanly.
